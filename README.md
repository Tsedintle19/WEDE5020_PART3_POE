## Project overview
CareerBridge SA is a suggested non-profit organisation that intends to connect high school learners with opportunities in higher education in South Africa. Numerous learners particularly those residing in rural and underprivileged areas cannot partake in career expos because of financial limitations, distance or insufficient transportation. Hence, they frequently overlook crucial facts regarding university admission, career options, scholarship, bursaries and application processes. CareerBridge SA aims to address this issue by developing an online platform that enables learners to access career advice and university information at given time via their mobile devices or computers.  The site will offer complimentary educational materials and online career fair experiences to assist learners in making wise decisions pertaining their future.
## Website Goals and Objectives
Goals
The site will:
•	Assist learners in discovering various professions
•	List the prerequisites for university admission
•	Share details regarding TVET colleges
•	Disclose scholarships or bursaries opportunities
•	Enable learners to participate in online career expos
Objectives
The redesigned website will:
•	Be simple to navigate on mobile devices and computers
•	Supply correct career details
•	Enable learners to explore universities and professions
•	Assist learners in determining their APS scores
•	Permit learners to reach out to career counsellors
