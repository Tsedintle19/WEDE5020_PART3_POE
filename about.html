About CareerBridge SA

Image source:unsplash

CareerBridge SA is a suggested non-profit organisation that intends to connect high school learners with opportunities in higher education in South Africa. The intension of this website is to offer students a free, user-friendly online platform to discover, explore career paths,understand university admission criteria, find scholarship or bursary options and engage in virtual career guidance activities from the comfort of their homes via mobile devices or computers.

Our Mission

To offer all South African learners free , dependable and accessible career advice and university information via digital technology

Our Vission

To guarantee that each learner irrespective of their location has the same access to job opportunities and higher education resources .

Our Impact

  • 50+ Career Resources
  • 30+ Virtual Events
  • 80+ University Partners
